About

Why Genesis exists

Real freedom requires two things: a say in how the world is governed, and a world worth governing. The institutions designed to protect both are under simultaneous strain.

The Genesis Institution exists to understand that strain — and to develop the research, frameworks, and solutions that can answer it.

We conduct original research, publish accessible analysis, and build the infrastructure for those determined to protect and enrich life on Earth.

We are a forming Massachusetts 501(c)(3) nonprofit, founded by researchers with deep roots in political theory, security studies, and environmental science.

◈

Political Security

The governance conditions under which freedom persists. Democratic resilience, participatory institutions, structural defenses against authoritarian capture.

◈

Environmental Security

The planetary conditions under which freedom persists. Climate stability, biosphere integrity, remediation at scale.

◈

The Intersection

Where these risks compound. How climate shocks create authoritarian openings — and how preparation can close them before the crisis arrives.

Research

Year-One agenda

Three programs. Five tracks. All oriented toward a single question: what does it take to protect and enrich life on Earth?

Program A

Political Security

Structural resistance to fascism

Conditions under which democratic societies preserve institutions against authoritarian capture — judicial independence, civil-society durability, comparative cases.

Direct democracy — feasibility and infrastructure

What scales, what doesn't, what's been tried. Sortition, citizens' assemblies, liquid democracy. Phase I maps the terrain; Phase II builds the blueprints.

Program B

Environmental Security

Multi-vector environmental remediation

A mapped landscape of credible interventions across atmospheric, soil, marine, freshwater, and biospheric vectors — with cost, scale, and risk profiles.

Desert terraforming as carbon capture

Rigorous feasibility study of land remediation and carbon sequestration through large-scale desert restoration. Honest about what the carbon math does and doesn't support.

Program C

Compound Risk

Climate shock and the authoritarian opening

Genesis's signature question. How environmental shocks — drought, displacement, food insecurity — create the conditions in which authoritarian movements consolidate power, and what democratic preparation can close that window before the crisis arrives.
